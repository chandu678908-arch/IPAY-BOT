## A. General Information
- iPAY connects supported wallets with eligible payment and receiving orders.
- Workflow: (1) Connect an eligible wallet and keep it active. (2) Check the wallet, recipient, exact amount and expiry before completing an order. (3) Provide the matching UTR and payment screenshot when requested, then review the status. (4) Review offer conditions and the 300.00 performance quota before requesting reward withdrawals.
- The guide is for new and existing users. The splash screen does not show registration or login steps.

## B. Supported Wallets
- PhonePe, Paytm, MobiKwik and Freecharge are listed for both placing and receiving/selling orders.
- Placing = eligible purchase/payment orders; receiving/selling = the receiving side.
- Keep wallets active and confirm the linked account can receive funds.
- Business Wallet eligibility: one reference lists PhonePe Business, Paytm Business and MobiKwik Business; another names Google Pay Business, Paytm Business and BharatPe Business. The definitive eligible list is [Confirmation required]. Direct all Business Wallet questions to human support.
- The guide does not demonstrate wallet-binding or receiving-order procedures.

## C. Payment Orders (Deposit)
- Steps: (1) Open Deposit — tap Deposit in the bottom navigation. (2) Choose the order — review the amount range, terms and applicable rate, then tap BUY. (3) Confirm the wallet — in Select Tool, select the wallet matching your linked account and tap Confirm. (4) Open the transfer — verify the recipient, exact amount and expiry time, then tap Go to transfer. (5) Complete the payment — check the provider screen against the order before authorizing. (6) Retain the proof — keep the successful payment screenshot and copy its UTR or transaction reference. (7) Return to the order — after payment is confirmed, return to iPAY and tap Paid. (8) Submit the proof — enter the matching UTR, upload that payment screenshot and tap Submit. (9) Check the status — review the order record.
- Payment accuracy: match the bound wallet, order amount and recipient. Complete payment before expiry, retain the receipt and submit proof for that same transaction.
- Pocket UPI is prohibited for these orders.
- If payment status is uncertain or the order has expired, request a status check before paying again. Do not submit a duplicate payment.

## D. Commission and Rewards
- Current rate: 4% on successfully completed eligible orders (example: ₹40 on ₹1,000).
- Extra ₹5 applies only to selected eligible activity orders.
- An older order screenshot shows 3%; confirm the live offer and full conditions.
- Five welcome tasks total ₹95: bind PhonePe UPI ₹10; join Telegram ₹15; complete a payment task ₹30; contact customer service ₹15; bind another listed UPI ₹25.
- Availability, eligibility, verification and claim timing are [Confirmation required].
- Do not promise rewards without checking their conditions.

## E. Referral System
- Inviter reward: ₹100. Invited friend reward: ₹50.
- The friend must register through the referral link/code and complete 10 qualifying orders the same day, within the one-day validity.
- Incomplete referrals expire without rewards.
- Current availability, qualifying-order definitions and exact expiry calculation are [Confirmation required]. No automatic credit time is established.
- The bot cannot generate referral links or check referral status.

## F. Withdrawal Support
- For a delayed withdrawal, check: receiving limit, whether UPI is enabled, whether the account can receive funds, Business Wallet eligibility and the wallet connection.
- Retain order/payment records and ask iPAY to check the pending withdrawal. These checks do not prove the cause of a delay.
- The poster suggests rebinding and retrying; those actions are unverified for a pending order. Avoid duplicate payments or withdrawals.
- Claims that funds are safe or processing will resume after an issue is resolved are not verified guarantees.
- Never promise instant withdrawal or a specific processing time unless officially confirmed.

## G. UPI Lock Issues
- Distinguish four wallet states: Disconnected, customer-service block, self-check lock, Running.
- Repeated payment failures, device changes, unusual activity and security checks are possible causes, not diagnoses. Read the instructions for the state shown.
- Self-check: tap the lock and read the in-app instructions. Send ₹1 to your own UPI only if instructed, then tap Self Check. If the lock remains, wait for the displayed countdown to finish and check whether it unlocks automatically. If still locked, contact customer service.
- The test amount and live conditions are [Confirmation required].
- Avoid repeated attempts while locked. A provider-level block may require help from your bank or wallet provider.
- Verification and timer expiry do not guarantee an unlock or completion time.

## H. Performance Quota (300.00)
- All reward funds can be withdrawn only after completing payment tasks totaling 300.00.
- The unit, eligible tasks, calculation method and any other withdrawal conditions are [Confirmation required].
- A quota value alone does not confirm that a particular withdrawal has settled.
- IMPORTANT: The quota is NOT presented as a separate payment or unlocking fee. No additional payment destination or fee instruction has been supplied. Never tell users to pay a separate unlocking fee.

## I. Password and OTP
- Signed-in password reset workflow: open Assets and tap Password. Enter and confirm the new password, then tap OTP. Complete the verification prompt and check the SMS sent to the registered number. Enter that OTP in the app and tap Submit. Then log in with the new password.
- The guide does not show the Forgot password route, account recovery requirements, password complexity rules or OTP limits. These are [Confirmation required].
- Use only the OTP received for your own password request and keep it private.
- NEVER ask a user to send their password, OTP or PIN to the bot or support.

## J. Important Payment Rules
- Use the signed-in wallet that matches the wallet bound to iPAY.
- Pay the exact order amount before the order expires.
- Do not use Pocket UPI for these orders.
- If status is uncertain, request a status check before paying again or submitting a duplicate withdrawal.
- Non-compliant operations can cause reward or withdrawal failures.
- Submit matching UTR and payment screenshot for the same transaction.

## K. Support and Limitations
- Official support contacts: @Ipay800 and @Roas668 (also @Ipay811).
- The bot cannot access a user's live iPay account, verify payment status, process withdrawals, unlock wallets, or guarantee a payout.
- Escalate account-specific, disputed, pending, locked, failed, Business Wallet, and unclear cases to human support.
- Promotional claims of instant withdrawal, 24/7 support and 100% security are unverified.
