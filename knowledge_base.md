# iPay knowledge base — source: user-uploaded I PAY User Guide PDF

## Brand and basic workflow
I PAY connects supported wallets with eligible payment and receiving orders. Users link a wallet, review an order, complete the required transaction, submit requested proof, and track status. Successfully completed eligible orders may earn the applicable commission or activity reward.

Workflow:
1. Connect an eligible wallet and keep it active.
2. Check the wallet, recipient, exact amount, and expiry before completing an order.
3. Provide the matching UTR and payment screenshot when requested, then review the status.
4. Review offer conditions and the 300.00 performance quota before requesting reward withdrawals.

## Supported wallets
PhonePe, Paytm, MobiKwik, and Freecharge are listed for both placing and receiving/selling orders.
Business Wallet eligibility is not definitively established in the guide. Different references list different Business Wallet providers, so direct all Business Wallet questions to human support.

## Commission and activity rewards
The supplied current rate in the guide is 4% on successfully completed eligible orders; example: ₹40 on ₹1,000. An extra ₹5 applies to selected eligible activity orders. An older order screenshot shows 3%, so the live offer and conditions must be confirmed.

## Welcome tasks
The guide lists five welcome tasks totaling ₹95:
- Bind PhonePe UPI: ₹10
- Join Telegram: ₹15
- Complete a payment task: ₹30
- Contact customer service: ₹15
- Bind another listed UPI: ₹25
Availability, eligibility, verification, and claim timing are marked as requiring confirmation.

## Referral offer
The supplied offer says ₹100 for the inviter and ₹50 for the invited friend after the friend registers through the link/code and completes 10 qualifying orders on the same day, within a one-day validity. Incomplete referrals expire without rewards. Current availability, qualifying-order definitions, and exact expiry calculation require confirmation. No automatic credit time is established. Mention only with this disclaimer.

## 300.00 performance quota
The guide states that reward funds can be withdrawn only after completing payment tasks totaling 300.00. The unit, qualifying tasks, calculation method, and any other withdrawal conditions require confirmation. This quota is NOT described as a separate payment or unlocking fee. Never tell users to pay a separate unlocking fee.

## Payment steps
1. Open Deposit.
2. Review amount range, terms, and applicable rate; tap BUY.
3. In Select Tool, select the wallet matching the linked account and tap Confirm.
4. Verify recipient, exact amount, and expiry; tap Go to transfer.
5. Complete the payment after checking provider screen against the order.
6. Keep successful payment screenshot and copy UTR/transaction reference.
7. Return to I PAY and tap Paid.
8. Enter matching UTR, upload that payment screenshot, and tap Submit.
9. Review order record/status.

The supplied notice prohibits Pocket UPI for these orders. Match the signed-in wallet to the wallet bound to I PAY. Pay the exact amount before expiry. If payment status is uncertain or the order has expired, request a status check before paying again.

## Password reset
The shown signed-in password workflow is: open Assets > Password, enter and confirm the new password, tap OTP, complete verification prompt, check SMS sent to registered number, enter that OTP in the app, tap Submit, then log in with the new password. The guide does not establish the Forgot Password route, account recovery requirements, password complexity rules, or OTP limits. Never ask a user to send their OTP to the bot or support.

## Delayed withdrawals and UPI locks
For a delayed withdrawal, check receiving limit, whether UPI is enabled, whether the account can receive funds, Business Wallet eligibility, and wallet connection. Retain order/payment records and ask I PAY to check the pending withdrawal. These checks do not prove the cause of delay. Avoid duplicate payments or withdrawals. No processing-time guarantee is verified.

Distinguish the wallet state shown in the app: Disconnected, customer-service block, self-check lock, or Running. Repeated failures, device changes, unusual activity, and security checks are possible causes, not diagnoses. Follow in-app instructions. The guide says a ₹1 transfer to the user's own UPI should be made only if the app instructs it. Avoid repeated attempts while locked. A provider-level block may require the bank/wallet provider. No unlock or completion time is guaranteed.

## Human support and limitations
Official support contacts supplied by the user: @Ipay800 and @Roas668.
The bot cannot access a user's live iPay account, verify payment status, process withdrawals, unlock wallets, or guarantee a payout.
Escalate account-specific, disputed, pending, locked, failed, Business Wallet, and unclear cases to human support.
Do not ask for passwords, OTPs, card/PIN details, or other authentication secrets.
