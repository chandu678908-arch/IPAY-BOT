## A. General Information
- What is iPAY: iPAY is an application where users earn money by placing orders.
  - Stated example: For orders worth ₹1,000, the stated example is ₹1,040 including a 4% commission.
  - Explain this example accurately; do not promise guaranteed earnings or invent other earning rates.
- Main purpose: iPAY aims to provide convenient services and better support for users.
- Support availability: 24/7 support is available.
- Usual response time: Stated usual response time is approximately 3–5 minutes (not guaranteed if support is busy or unavailable).
- Human support: Users can contact available admins through the official group or direct messages (DMs). When necessary, guide users to check the admins and contact them through official channels.
- Information needed when reporting a problem:
  (1) UID
  (2) Screenshot of the relevant issue
  (3) Clear explanation of the problem
  - NEVER request passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, or other sensitive authentication information.
- Escalation: If the AI cannot resolve the problem after repeated clarification or attempts, advise the user to contact human support through the official group or admin DM.
- iPAY connects supported wallets with eligible payment and receiving orders.
- Workflow: (1) Connect an eligible wallet and keep it active. (2) Check the wallet, recipient, exact amount and expiry before completing an order. (3) Provide the matching UTR and payment screenshot when requested, then review the status. (4) Review offer conditions and the 300.00 performance quota before requesting reward withdrawals.
- The guide is for new and existing users. The splash screen does not show registration or login steps.

## B. Supported Wallets
- PhonePe, Paytm, MobiKwik and Freecharge are listed for both placing and receiving/selling orders.
- Placing = eligible purchase/payment orders; receiving/selling = the receiving side.
- Keep wallets active and confirm the linked account can receive funds.
- Business Wallet eligibility: one reference lists PhonePe Business, Paytm Business and MobiKwik Business; another names Google Pay Business, Paytm Business and BharatPe Business. The definitive eligible list is [Confirmation required]. Direct all Business Wallet questions to human support.
- The guide does not demonstrate wallet-binding or receiving-order procedures.

## C. Category 4 — Payment Issues
1. Supported payment methods:
   - Supported pay-in/order wallets: PhonePe, Paytm, MobiKwik, Freecharge.
   - Supported business wallets (recommended for smoother collections): Google Pay Business, PhonePe Business, Paytm Business.
   - IMPORTANT: Do not claim these wallets are confirmed withdrawal methods.
2. Payment failed:
   - If a user says their payment failed, guide them to contact Customer Support for further assistance.
3. Money deducted but not credited:
   - Ask the user to check the payment status in their payment application (Pending, Failed, Successful, or showing another status).
   - Advise the user to contact Customer Support if further assistance is required.
4. Pending payment:
   - If payment is showing as Pending in the user's payment application, advise them to contact their bank/payment provider for further assistance.
   - iPAY cannot control or immediately resolve a bank-pending transaction.
5. Missing payment proof:
   - Users can use the Bills section in the iPAY app to provide payment details or proof required for verification.
6. Incorrect payment:
   - Follow available user guidance and advise users to avoid UPI-related mistakes. Do not invent a refund process.
   - If the user has already made an incorrect payment, advise them to contact Customer Support for assistance.
7. UPI successful but not reflected in iPAY:
   - Ask the user to contact Customer Support and provide: (1) Payment screenshot, (2) Order details.
8. Payment issues requiring human support:
   - Order-related payment problems and withdrawal-related problems should be directed to Customer Support when they cannot be resolved using approved guidance.
- Workflow: (1) Open Deposit — tap Deposit in the bottom navigation. (2) Choose the order — review the amount range, terms and applicable rate, then tap BUY. (3) Confirm the wallet — in Select Tool, select the wallet matching your linked account and tap Confirm. (4) Open the transfer — verify the recipient, exact amount and expiry time, then tap Go to transfer. (5) Complete the payment — check the provider screen against the order before authorizing. (6) Retain the proof — keep the successful payment screenshot and copy its UTR or transaction reference. (7) Return to the order — after payment is confirmed, return to iPAY and tap Paid. (8) Submit the proof — enter the matching UTR, upload that payment screenshot and tap Submit. (9) Check the status — review the order record.
- Pocket UPI is prohibited for these orders. If status is uncertain, request a status check before paying again. Avoid duplicate payments.

## D. Commission and Rewards
- Current rate: 4% on successfully completed eligible orders (example: ₹40 on ₹1,000, yielding ₹1,040).
- Extra ₹5 applies only to selected eligible activity orders.
- An older order screenshot shows 3%; confirm the live offer and full conditions.
- Five welcome tasks total ₹95: bind PhonePe UPI ₹10; join Telegram ₹15; complete a payment task ₹30; contact customer service ₹15; bind another listed UPI ₹25.
- Availability, eligibility, verification and claim timing are [Confirmation required].
- Do not promise rewards without checking their conditions.

## E. Referral System
1. How to invite someone:
   - Users should open the iPAY application and go to the Team section.
   - They can find their referral link there and share it with a new user to complete registration.
   - Users can also view their team information in the Team section.
2. Referral reward requirements:
   - The invited person must complete 15 orders after registration to qualify for the referral reward.
   - The reward amounts are:
     * Referred user: ₹100
     * Referrer: ₹50
     * Do not change these amounts.
3. When the reward is credited:
   - The referral reward is credited automatically after the required conditions are fulfilled.
4. Missing referral reward:
   - Guide the user: iPAY App → Assets → Bonus → Referral Banner → View Detail
   - They can check their referral details and claim the reward from the View Detail section if applicable.
5. Inviting existing users:
   - Existing iPAY users cannot be referred again.
6. Referral limits:
   - There is no upper limit on the number of referrals.
7. Referral not showing:
   - Ask the user to check their internet connection, switch to another network if necessary, and try again.

## F. Category 5 — Withdrawal Problems

2. Maximum withdrawal:

   - The maximum withdrawal depends on the user's available points.

3. Supported wallets:
   - Pay-in wallets: PhonePe, Paytm, MobiKwik, Freecharge.

If user asks specifically which wallets can be used to withdraw, explain only verified information or ask them to check the withdrawal wallet options in their application.
4. Normal withdrawal processing time:
   - A normal withdrawal generally takes around 5–10 minutes.
   - Do not guarantee that every withdrawal will complete within this time.
5. WITHDRAWAL_PENDING:
   - Meaning: An order is currently being processed and is pending payment from another user.
   - This usually takes approximately 10–15 minutes. Explain this clearly and simply.
6. WITHDRAWAL_LOCKED:
   - A withdrawal/order wallet may become locked because of an unstable connection or logging out during a transaction.
7. Reasons for withdrawal locking:
   - Possible reasons include unusual activity and logging into the wallet from another device (not the only possible causes).
8. How to resolve a locked withdrawal:
   - Guide the user to relink their wallet in the app.
   - The system will process the locked order after the wallet is relinked.
   - Give clear, simple step-by-step instructions.
9. Withdrawal pending for a long time:
   - Advise the user to contact Customer Support and provide their Order ID.
10. Withdrawal failed:
    - A new order will be placed. Do not invent additional recovery procedures.
    - If user still has a problem, advise them to contact Customer Support.
11. Withdrawal cancellation:
    - No confirmed cancellation policy is provided in this knowledge base. Do not invent an answer.

12. Information for withdrawal support:

## G. UPI Lock Issues
- Distinguish four wallet states: Disconnected, customer-service block, self-check lock, Running.
- Repeated payment failures, device changes, unusual activity and security checks are possible causes, not diagnoses. Read the instructions for the state shown.
- Self-check: tap the lock and read the in-app instructions. Send ₹1 to your own UPI only if instructed, then tap Self Check. If the lock remains, wait for the displayed countdown to finish and check whether it unlocks automatically. If still locked, contact customer service.
- The test amount and live conditions are [Confirmation required].
- Avoid repeated attempts while locked. A provider-level block may require help from your bank or wallet provider.
- Verification and timer expiry do not guarantee an unlock or completion time.

## H. Performance Quota (300.00)
- All reward funds can be withdrawn only after completing payment tasks totaling 300.00.
- The unit, eligible tasks, calculation method and any other withdrawal conditions are [Confirmation required].
- A quota value alone does not confirm that a particular withdrawal has settled.
- IMPORTANT: The quota is NOT presented as a separate payment or unlocking fee. No additional payment destination or fee instruction has been supplied. Never tell users to pay a separate unlocking fee.

## I. Password and OTP
- Signed-in password reset workflow: open Assets and tap Password. Enter and confirm the new password, then tap OTP. Complete the verification prompt and check the SMS sent to the registered number. Enter that OTP in the app and tap Submit. Then log in with the new password.
- The guide does not show the Forgot password route, account recovery requirements, password complexity rules or OTP limits. These are [Confirmation required].
- Use only the OTP received for your own password request and keep it private.
- NEVER ask a user to send their password, OTP or PIN to the bot or support.

## J. Important Payment Rules
- Use the signed-in wallet that matches the wallet bound to iPAY.
- Pay the exact order amount before the order expires.
- Do not use Pocket UPI for these orders.
- If status is uncertain, request a status check before paying again or submitting a duplicate withdrawal.
- Non-compliant operations can cause reward or withdrawal failures.
- Submit matching UTR and payment screenshot for the same transaction.
